# Integration across pillars (agreed 2026-10-18)

*Applies to both Pillar I (market consequences) and Pillar II (consequential process modelling). Replaces Pillar II's "Synthesis — Effect of refinement across types of cLCA" as a separate objective.*

Integration is a cross-cutting element with no dedicated HQP and no end-of-program work package. A synthesis that waits for every PhD to finish is fragile, so integration is built into the design.

1. **Shared protocol from year 1.** Both pillars use the same perturbation definition, the same two success criteria (process- or market-level agreement within a stated tolerance, and preservation of LCA/decision rankings) and the same reporting template. Each finished case adds a row to a cumulative comparison table, so results are comparable by construction.
2. **Shared cases carry the integration.** Steel (ArcelorMittal) is studied by one MSc from each pillar (MSc I on O1.3, hindcast; MSc II on O2.3, class benchmark). Heavy-industry heat in EnergyScope Québec joins O1.1 (PhD I) with the CANMET project. Nitrogen builds on results of the PLASMA project (plasma fixation vs Haber-Bosch), which runs until 2027 and funds no HQP in this program: PhD III (O2.2, anticipatory phase, years 4–5) and MSc III (O1.2, years 4–5) study it at the same time. The joint variance decomposition (deployed foreground configuration vs background marginal supplier) comes out as co-authored papers on these cases, with PhD III owning the foreground side on nitrogen.
3. **Two PI-led synthesis moments.** Mid-term (year 3), from the first completed MSc cases; year 5, with the PhD students as co-authors.
4. **One program-level secondary question**, replacing the pillar-level ones: *How much do representation choices change cLCA conclusions across decision types, at the market level (which units respond) and at the process level (how they operate)?*

No postdoc is planned for synthesis. If one became available, its role would be mid-program (years 2–4), maintaining the shared protocol.

## HQP allocation and calendar (agreed 2026-10-18)

Six HQP funded by Discovery across both pillars: 3 PhD (4 years) and 3 MSc (2 years). MSc projects apply and test what the PhDs develop (protocol, class interface), so none starts in year 1.

**Numbering.** HQP are numbered by order of start, not by objective, because one PhD can carry two objectives (PhD I covers O1.1 in both its core and Tier A roles; PhD III covers both phases of O2.2). PhD I = O1.1, PhD II = O2.1, PhD III = O2.2; MSc I = O1.3, MSc II = O2.3, MSc III = O1.2. Objectives keep their O-codes.

- **Year 1:** two PhDs, each with independent early output (PhD II: database diagnostic, O2.1; PhD I: first EnergyScope perturbations, O1.1).
- **Nitrogen pair** (PhD III, anticipatory phase of O2.2 + MSc III, O1.2) runs in years 4–5 and feeds the final synthesis.
- **Steel pair** (MSc I, O1.3 + MSc II, O2.3) starts together in year 2 and finishes in time for the mid-term synthesis. Another metallurgy sector can replace steel if needed.
- **Peak of 5 in years 2–3:** smooth with scholarships (FRQNT, NSERC CGS); undergraduate trainees can support diagnostic screening and pipeline audits.
- **Training plan:** update "two-thirds PhD" to about half PhD and half MSc.

**Linked activities, not Discovery HQP:** the PLASMA project, as collaborative context until 2027 (it funds a postdoc, no HQP of this program); the CANMET project; Arianne Provost-Savard (external collaborator, postdoc elsewhere).

**Group expertise, not case studies:** the ongoing plastics PhD (MFA, OPLA ecodesign tool, early AI-assisted scale-up) is preliminary work the program builds on. Plastics are not among the program's case studies, and that PhD is not counted as Discovery HQP.

**Pillar II structure (updated 2026-10-18):** three objectives, each with a funded student. O2.1 representation and database diagnostic (PhD II); O2.2 AI-assisted construction and anticipation, from known processes (composting, HTC) to the plasma nitrogen case (PhD III); O2.3 steel class benchmark (MSc II). The former emerging-technology objective is folded into PhD III.
